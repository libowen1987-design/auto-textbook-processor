1 Introduction
> **第1节 引言**：植被冠层的电磁散射已有大量基于矢量辐射传输理论（第一卷第4章）的研究。经典辐射传输假设粒子独立散射，但当粒子相对位置随机性小于波长时（如树枝和叶子的簇状结构），该假设失效。此时需考虑集体散射效应和相关散射。
Various theoretical models have been developed to characterize the electro-
magnetic wave scattering properties of vegetation canopies. In the past, wave
scattering from vegetation has been studied extensively with vector radiative
transfer theory as described in Volume I. Classical radiative transfer theory
assumes that the particles scatter independently so that the scattering phase
functions add. This assumption is based on the random phase of scattering
by different particles and is valid if the particle positions are independent
and the randomness of relative positions is comparable to or larger than a
wavelength. However, such an assumption can be invalid for microwave scat-
tering of certain cases of vegetation canopy where the randomness of their
relative positions is less than a wavelength. For example, branches and leaves
in a tree occur in clusters, and there are correlations between their relative
positions. Scatterers with this kind of cluster structure can demonstrate col-
lective scattering effects. In Chapter 7, we have shown by simulations of
point scatterers the effects of clustering. Collective scattering effects include
correlated scattering and take into account the relative phase of scattered
waves from the scatterers and their neighbors. The mutual coherent wave
interactions between scatterers are also to be included.
> 第7章已展示点散射体模拟中的聚集效应。集体散射效应包括相关散射，考虑散射体及其邻近散射体之间的相对相位，以及散射体之间的互相干波相互作用。
In numerical solutions of Maxwell's equations for random media, the po-
sitions and characteristics of the scatterers are randomly generated accord-
ing to prescribed statistics. We have described procedures for generating 2-D
and 3-D dense media in Chapters 8 and 9. For the case of vegetation, the
scattering by correlated scatterers was first studied by using the coherent
addition approximation (CAA). The probability density functions of positions
are introduced. However, it is difficult to calculate the probability density
functions and the pair distribution functions for natural vegetation. Coher-
ent scattering effects including the relative structures of branches and leaves
draw important attention [Yueh et al. 1992; Au et al. 1994, 1996]. To build
realistic tree structures, Lindenmayer systems can be used [Prusinkiewicz
and Lindenmayer, 1990]. Subsequently coherent scattering models are de-
veloped for these Lindenmayer systems. [Chen et al. 1995, 1996; Zhang et.
al. 1996; Le Toan et al. 1997; Lin and Sarabandi, 1999a,b].
> 在随机介质的Maxwell方程数值解中，散射体的位置和特性按预设统计参数随机生成。第8、9章描述了2D和3D稠密介质的生成方法。对于植被，相关散射体的散射首先通过相干叠加近似（CAA）研究。引入了位置的概率密度函数，但对于自然植被，计算PDF和配对分布函数非常困难。Lindenmayer系统（L系统）可用于构建逼真的树木结构。

graphics for realistic visualization of plant structures and growth process. In
Section 3, we first describe coherent scattering by a particle in the presence of
a boundary. Then we calculate the scattering amplitude from a layer of trees
overlaying a flat-ground surface by using coherent addition approximation
and compare it to the independent scattering approximation and the tree-
independent scattering approximation. The coherent addition approximation
takes into account the relative phase shifts between scatterers in a realization
of trees. The tree-independent scattering approximation considers every tree
as an independent scatterer. Under the tree-independent approximation, the
scattering cross section of a forest is the sum of scattering cross sections from
each tree. On the other hand, under the independent scattering approxima-
tion, the scattering cross section of a forest is the sum of the scattering cross
sections from cach branch. The numerical results of backscattering coeffi-
cients for a layer of trees overlaying a flat-ground surface are illustrated as a
function of frequency. The difference of the results by different approxima-
tions increases as frequency decreases. At the P-band, there can be a 20-dB
difference between the result of coherent addition approximation and that
of the independent scattering approximation.

scattering and absorption on coherent wave propagation within the veg-
etation canopy. In Section 5, we calculate the scattering coefficients from
a layer of tree structures overlaying a flat-ground surface by using the dis-
crete dipole approximation method and compare it to the independent
scattering approximation and to the coherent addition approximation. The
discrete dipole approximation is the volume integral approach described in
Chapter 2, Section 3. The advantage of this approach is that the mutual
interactions between the branches are included. Tt is found that there can
be a 2-dB absorption enhancement and up to 17-dB scattering enhance-
ment for cross-polarization. The difference between the discrete dipole
approximation and the coherent addition approximation is due to the co-
herent mutual interactions among the branches within a tree. Lastly, the
coherent addition approximation is applied to the numerical simulation of
C-band spaceborne radar response for characterizing the rice growth and
comparing with the ERS-1 backscatter data [Le Toan et al. 1997].

2 Plant Modeling by Using L-Systems
> **第2节：使用L系统进行植物建模**。植被冠层通常包含叶片、枝条、茎杆等各类元素，其分布非均匀随机，而是在植物或树木中局部聚集。
A vegetation canopy generally consists of many types of vegetative elements,
such as leaves, branches, stems, and stalks. The distribution of leaves and
branches is not uniformly random, they actually occur in random clusters
locally in a plant or tree. A major problem in assessing their influence on the
propagation of electromagnetic waves is to model their structurally complex
and time-varying properties. However, recent mathematical advances have
provided useful means for plant modeling based on fractals [Mandelbrot,
1983) or Lindenmayer systems (L-systems) {Prusinkiewicz and Lindenmayer,
1990}. In the modeling of vegetation, L-systems have proven to be successful
from a practical perspective. In the remote sensing research, plant models
generated by L-systems have been applied to study the transport of light in
vegetation canopies [Goel et al. 1991]. For microwave and millimeter waves,
the use of L-systems is important because one can then include coherent
addition and coherent wave interaction effects in a vegetation canopy. In
this section we describe the L-systems method and use this technique to
generate trees with different structures.
> L系统由Lindenmayer于1968年提出，是一种描述简单生物系统发育和生长的数学形式化方法。其核心思想是重写（rewriting）：通过一组重写规则（产生式）反复替换简单初始对象的部分来定义复杂对象。在L系统中，重写规则并行应用，可递归进行。
The L-systems, introduced by Lindenmayer {1968], are a mathemat-
ical formalism for describing the development and growth of simple liv-
ing systems. The original emphasis is on plant topology. Further develop-
ment [Prusinkiewicz and Lindenmayer, 1990] takes into account the rules
of describing plant development in time, and the distinctive feature of self-
similarity, as characterized by Mandelbrot [1983], that is kept in the rewriting
process of L-systems. Rewriting is the central idea in L-systems for defining
complex objects by successively replacing parts of a simple initial object us-
ing a set of rewriting rules or productions. In L-systems the rewriting rules
are applied in parallel and can be carried out recursively in the growth pro-
cess.
> L系统已应用于多种领域。本节的介绍紧密遵循Prusinkiewicz和Lindenmayer[1990]的工作。
L-systems have been utilized for a variety of applications. The descrip-
tion of L-systems presented in this section follows closely the work of
Prusinkiewicz and Lindenmayer [1990]. For more details, we refer the
readers to the existing literature [Herman et al. 1975; Mandelbrot, 1983;
Prusinkiewicz and Hanan, 1989; Prusinkiewicz and Lindenmayer, 1990].

2.3 Computer Simulations of Stochastic L-Systems and Input

> **2.3 随机L系统的计算机模拟和输入文件**。目前有多种商业和共享软件可用于L系统模拟。例如 $lsys$ 是Prusinkiewicz和Lindenmayer[1990]开发的植物生成程序，由Jonathan Leech用C++编写，运行于UNIX系统。

possible. A variety of commercial software and shareware are currently avail-

{Prusinkiewicz and Lindenmayer, 1990]. The code Isys was written by

desigued for manipulating production L-systems. In addition to specifying

widths of all branches generated by L-systems can be obtained by this tool.
This software is capable of simulating various examples presented in The Al-
gorithmic Beauty of Plants. In the following examples, we will demonstrate
how to model plants by using this tool.
