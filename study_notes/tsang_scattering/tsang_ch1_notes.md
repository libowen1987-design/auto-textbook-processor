3 Scattering by Cylinders in the Presence of Two Reflective
Boundaries
In this section, we study the multiple scattering by cylinders placed inside
a parallel plate waveguide. The problem is conveniently formulated in terms
of the dyadic Green’s function of vector cylindrical waves and expressed
in terms of waveguide modes. ‘The case considered here resembles the cou-
pling problem among imultiple vias in high frequency circuits. The Foldy-Lax
multiple scattering equations are derived using vector cylindrical waves and
waveguide inode indices as basis functions. It is important to recognize that
the Foldy-Lax equations are decoupled among the waveguide modes. In terms
of the matrix equation, this decoupling reduces significantly the dimension
of the matrix equation as one only needs to solve Foldy-Lax equation for
