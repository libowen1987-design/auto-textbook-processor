# Chapter 14: Modeling of Antennas
> **中英双语版**

> 天线建模

## 14.1 Introduction
> 引言

FDTD is uniquely suited for antenna analysis: (1) wideband capability from a single simulation via Fourier transform, (2) natural handling of complex materials and geometries, (3) direct computation of near and far fields, and (4) time-domain visualization of transient behavior.
> FDTD 特别适合天线分析：(1) 通过傅里叶变换从单次仿真获得宽带特性，(2) 自然处理复杂材料和几何结构，(3) 直接计算近场和远场，(4) 瞬态行为的时域可视化。

**Key elements**: (a) antenna geometry model, (b) feed model (transmission line or gap), (c) ABC/PML termination, (d) near-to-far-field (NTFF) transformation, (e) input impedance and pattern extraction.
> **关键要素**：(a) 天线几何模型，(b) 馈电模型（传输线或间隙馈电），(c) 吸收边界/PML 截断，(d) 近场-远场变换，(e) 输入阻抗和方向图提取。

## 14.2 Transmitting and Receiving Antennas
> 发射和接收天线

### 14.2.1 Transmitting Mode
> 发射模式

The antenna is placed near the center of the computational volume, fed by a modeled transmission line. A virtual surface S' encloses the antenna for NTFF transformation (Huygens' principle):
> 天线放置在计算区域中心附近，由建模的传输线馈电。虚拟表面 S' 包围天线用于 NTFF 变换（惠更斯原理）：
$$
\mathbf{E}^r(\mathbf{r}, t) = \frac{1}{4\pi r} \iint_{S'} \left[ \hat{r} \times \hat{r} \times \frac{\partial}{\partial t} (\hat{n}' \times \mathbf{H}) - \frac{\hat{r}}{ \eta_0} \times \frac{\partial}{\partial t} (\hat{n}' \times \mathbf{E}) \right]_{t = t'} dS'
$$
where $t' = t - |\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}'|/c$ is the retarded time.
> 其中 $t' = t - |\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}'|/c$ 为推迟时间。

### 14.2.2 Receiving Mode
> 接收模式

A virtual surface creates a total-field/scattered-field region. The incident plane wave is generated by electric and magnetic surface currents:
> 虚拟表面创建总场/散射场区域。入射平面波由电场和磁场表面电流产生：
$$
\mathbf{J}_s(\mathbf{r}, t) = -\hat{n} \times \mathbf{H}^{\text{inc}}, \quad \mathbf{M}_s(\mathbf{r}, t) = \hat{n} \times \mathbf{E}^{\text{inc}}
$$

### 14.2.3 Symmetry Reduction
> 对称性缩减

Using PEC/PMC symmetry planes can reduce the computational volume by 8× (3 symmetry planes). Example: bow-tie antenna with image plane + two PMC symmetry planes reduces volume 8:1.
> 使用理想导体/理想磁壁对称面可将计算量减少 8 倍（3 个对称面）。示例：带镜像面的蝶形天线加两个 PMC 对称面可将体积缩减至 1/8。

## 14.3 Antenna Feed Models
> 天线馈电模型

### 14.3.1 Detailed Feed
> 详细馈电模型

Full coaxial line modeling (Fig. 14.5a): conical monopole with matching ring achieved -46 dB reflection. For rotationally symmetric feeds, BOR-FDTD (Ch12) is used.
> 完整同轴线建模：带匹配环的锥形单极子实现了 -46 dB 反射。对于旋转对称馈电，使用 BOR-FDTD（第12章）。

### 14.3.2 Simple Gap Feed
> 简单间隙馈电

For a monopole over an image plane, the feed is a single-cell gap between the wire and ground. Applied voltage $V_g(t)$:
> 对于镜像平面上的单极子，馈电是导线与地面之间的单个网格间隙。施加电压 $V_g(t)$：
$$
E_z^{\text{gap}} = \frac{V_g(t)}{\Delta z}
$$

Feedpoint current via Ampère's law around the wire:
> 馈电点电流通过导线周围的安培环路定律：
$$
I^{n+1/2} = \Delta x \left[ H_x(i_a, j_a-1/2) - H_x(i_a, j_a+1/2) \right] + \Delta y \left[ H_y(i_a+1/2, j_a) - H_y(i_a-1/2, j_a) \right]
$$

Input impedance: $Z(\omega) = V(\omega) / I(\omega)$ via Fourier transform.
> 输入阻抗：通过傅里叶变换得到 $Z(\omega) = V(\omega) / I(\omega)$。

### 14.3.3 Transmission Line Feed
> 传输线馈电

Instead of detailed coaxial modeling, a 1D transmission line is attached to the antenna at the feed point. The 1D FDTD line has characteristic impedance $Z_0$ and propagates:
> 代替详细同轴线建模，在馈电点处将一维传输线连接到天线。一维 FDTD 线具有特性阻抗 $Z_0$：
$$
\frac{\partial V}{\partial z} = -L \frac{\partial I}{\partial t}, \quad \frac{\partial I}{\partial z} = -C \frac{\partial V}{\partial t}
$$

At the antenna junction: $V_{\text{ant}} = V_{\text{line}}$ and $I_{\text{ant}} = I_{\text{line}}$.
> 在天线连接处：$V_{\text{ant}} = V_{\text{line}}$ 且 $I_{\text{ant}} = I_{\text{line}}$。

## 14.4 Near-to-Far-Field (NTFF) Transformation
> 近场-远场变换

### Time-Domain Form
> 时域形式

Records time-domain fields on a virtual surface, then applies:
> 在虚拟表面上记录时域场，然后应用：
$$
\mathbf{E}^{\text{far}}(\theta, \phi, t) = \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \left[ \mathbf{F}_E(\theta, \phi, t) \right] * \frac{1}{r \cdot c}
$$
where $\mathbf{F}_E$ is the time-domain electric radiation vector computed from surface currents.
> 其中 $\mathbf{F}_E$ 是从表面电流计算得到的时域电辐射矢量。

### Frequency-Domain Form
> 频域形式

Equivalent but performed via FFT:
> 等价但通过 FFT 执行：
$$
\mathbf{E}^{\text{far}}(\theta, \phi, \omega) = \frac{j\omega e^{-jkr}}{4\pi r} \left[ \eta_0 \hat{r} \times \hat{r} \times \mathbf{N}(\omega) + \hat{r} \times \mathbf{L}(\omega) \right]
$$
where $\mathbf{N}(\omega) = \iint_{S'} \hat{n} \times \mathbf{H}(\omega) e^{j k \hat{r} \cdot \mathbf{r}'} dS'$ and $\mathbf{L}(\omega) = \iint_{S'} \hat{n} \times \mathbf{E}(\omega) e^{j k \hat{r} \cdot \mathbf{r}'} dS'$.
> 其中 $\mathbf{N}(\omega)$ 为电辐射矢量，$\mathbf{L}(\omega)$ 为磁辐射矢量。

## 14.5 Case Studies
> 案例研究

### 14.5.1 Standard-Gain Horn (Case Study I, §14.6)
> 标准增益喇叭天线

WR-90 waveguide-fed pyramidal horn (10 dB gain at 10 GHz). FDTD results:
> WR-90 波导馈电角锥喇叭（10 GHz 处增益 10 dB）。FDTD 结果：
- $|S_{11}| < -20$ dB across 8-12 GHz
  > 在 8-12 GHz 范围内 $|S_{11}| < -20$ dB
- E/H-plane patterns within 0.5 dB of measurements
  > E/H 面方向图与测量值偏差在 0.5 dB 以内
- Gain: 10.2 dB at 10 GHz (measurement: 10.0 dB)
  > 增益：10 GHz 处 10.2 dB（测量值：10.0 dB）

### 14.5.2 Vivaldi Slotline Array (Case Study II, §14.7)
> Vivaldi 槽线阵列

Tapered slot antenna array with 4 elements:
> 4 单元渐变槽天线阵列：
- Wideband operation: 3-11 GHz (10:1 bandwidth)
  > 宽带工作：3-11 GHz（10:1 带宽）
- Scan impedance for $\pm 45^\circ$ beam steering
  > $\pm 45^\circ$ 波束扫描的扫描阻抗
- Mutual coupling between elements: $|S_{21}| < -15$ dB across band
  > 单元间互耦：全频段 $|S_{21}| < -15$ dB

### 14.5.3 Bow-Tie Antenna
> 蝶形天线

Thin-wire bow-tie on ground plane:
> 接地平面上的细线蝶形天线：
- FDTD with thin-wire subcell model (§10.5)
  > 使用细线亚网格模型的 FDTD（§10.5）
- Input impedance: 50-80 $\Omega$ across 1-3 GHz
  > 输入阻抗：1-3 GHz 范围内 50-80 $\Omega$
- Pattern: nearly omni-directional in H-plane
  > 方向图：H 面近似全向

## 14.6 Case Study: Vivaldi Slotline Array (Detailed)
> 案例研究：Vivaldi 槽线阵列（详细）

The Vivaldi tapered slot antenna (TSA) achieves multi-octave bandwidth through a gradual transition from a slotline to a radiating aperture. FDTD analysis of a 4-element Vivaldi array ($\S14.7$) involved:
> Vivaldi 渐变槽天线通过从槽线到辐射口径的渐变过渡实现多倍频程带宽。4 单元 Vivaldi 阵列的 FDTD 分析涉及：
- **Unit cell size**: $\lambda_0/30$ at the highest frequency (15 GHz)
  > **网格单元尺寸**：最高频率（15 GHz）处 $\lambda_0/30$
- **Total cells**: $200 \times 150 \times 50 = 1.5$ million cells
  > **总网格数**：150 万
- **Time-steps**: 10,000 (sufficient for $S_{11}$ convergence)
  > **时间步数**：10,000（足以使 $S_{11}$ 收敛）
- **Results**: Active $S_{11} < -10$ dB across 3-12 GHz for scan angles up to $45^\circ$
  > **结果**：扫描角达 $45^\circ$ 时，3-12 GHz 范围内有源 $S_{11} < -10$ dB
- **Mutual coupling**: $|S_{21}| < -15$ dB between adjacent elements
  > **互耦**：相邻单元间 $|S_{21}| < -15$ dB

## 14.7 Near-Field to Near-Field Transformation
> 近场到近场变换

For scenarios requiring field values at points too far for direct FDTD computation but too close for the Fraunhofer approximation, the near-to-near-field transformation is used. Given fields on a surface $S'$, the field at an external point $\mathbf{r}$ is:
> 对于需要场值点距离太远无法直接 FDTD 计算、又太近不满足 Fraunhofer 近似的情况，使用近场到近场变换。给定表面 $S'$ 上的场：
$$
\mathbf{E}(\mathbf{r}, t) = \frac{1}{4\pi} \iint_{S'} \left[ \frac{\hat{n}' \times \mathbf{E}(\mathbf{r}', t_r)}{R^3} + \frac{\hat{n}' \times \dot{\mathbf{E}}(\mathbf{r}', t_r)}{c R^2} \right] \times \mathbf{R} \, dS' + \text{(magnetic terms)}
$$
where $\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}'$, $R = |\mathbf{R}|$, and $t_r = t - R/c$.
> 其中 $\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}'$，$R = |\mathbf{R}|$，$t_r = t - R/c$。

## 14.8 Antenna Coupling and EMC
> 天线耦合与电磁兼容

FDTD directly computes mutual coupling between antennas in an array:
> FDTD 直接计算阵列中天线间的互耦：
$$
S_{pq} = \frac{V_q^-(\omega)}{V_p^+(\omega)}
$$

For EMC applications, FDTD can compute:
> 对于电磁兼容应用，FDTD 可计算：
- **Shielding effectiveness** of enclosures with aperture arrays
  > 带孔阵列机箱的屏蔽效能
- **Cable coupling** from external fields
  > 外部场引起的线缆耦合
- **Radiation from PCB traces** 
  > PCB 走线的辐射

A typical EMC FDTD simulation: enclosure with slot array, $\lambda_0/20$ cell size, PML boundaries, Gaussian pulse excitation — yields SE across 0.5-18 GHz in a single run.
> 典型 EMC FDTD 仿真：带槽阵列的机箱，$\lambda_0/20$ 网格尺寸，PML 边界，高斯脉冲激励——单次运行获得 0.5-18 GHz 的屏蔽效能。

## Key Takeaways
> 关键要点

1. **Single FDTD simulation** yields wideband antenna performance via Fourier transform.
   > **单次 FDTD 仿真**通过傅里叶变换获得宽带天线性能。
2. **Gap feed** is simplest but requires careful current integration.
   > **间隙馈电**最简单，但需要仔细的电流积分。
3. **NTFF transformation** is essential for far-field computation.
   > **NTFF 变换**对远场计算至关重要。
4. **Symmetry** reduces memory by up to 8×.
   > **对称性**最多可减少 8 倍内存。
5. **Thin-wire model** drastically reduces mesh requirements for wire antennas.
   > **细线模型**大幅减少线天线的网格需求。
6. **Mutual coupling** and **array scan impedance** are naturally obtained from FDTD.
   > **互耦**和**阵列扫描阻抗**可从 FDTD 自然获得。
7. **Antenna coupling/EMC** simulations benefit from FDTD's broadband single-run capability.
   > **天线耦合/EMC**仿真受益于 FDTD 的单次运行宽带能力。
