# Sadiku《Elements of Electromagnetics》Chapter 6: Magnetostatic Fields
> **中英双语版**

Biot-Savart law, Ampère's law, magnetic flux, scalar and vector potentials, magnetic dipoles.

## 6.1 Biot-Savart Law | 毕奥-萨伐尔定律
$$d\mathbf{H} = \frac{I\,d\mathbf{l} \times \mathbf{a}_R}{4\pi R^2}, \quad \mathbf{H} = \frac{I}{4\pi} \oint \frac{d\mathbf{l} \times \mathbf{a}_R}{R^2}$$
Magnetic field due to a current element / 电流元产生的磁场。

## 6.2 Ampère's Circuital Law | 安培环路定律
$$\oint \mathbf{H} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = I_{\text{enc}}$$
Applies to infinite line current, coaxial cable, toroid, infinite current sheet.

## 6.3 Magnetic Flux Density | 磁通密度
$$\mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{H} \quad (\text{free space}), \quad \Phi = \int_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{S}$$

## 6.4 Gauss's Law for Magnetism | 磁高斯定律
$$\oint_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = 0 \quad (\text{no magnetic monopoles / 无磁单极})$$

## 6.5 Magnetic Vector Potential | 磁矢量位
$$\mathbf{B} = \nabla \times \mathbf{A}, \quad \nabla^2 \mathbf{A} = -\mu_0 \mathbf{J}$$

## 6.6 Magnetic Dipole | 磁偶极子
$$\mathbf{m} = I S \mathbf{a}_n \quad (\text{magnetic dipole moment / 磁矩})$$

## 6.7 Magnetization | 磁化
$$\mathbf{B} = \mu_0(\mathbf{H} + \mathbf{M}), \quad \mu_r = 1 + \chi_m, \quad \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mu_r \mathbf{H}$$

## 6.8 Magnetic Boundary Conditions / 磁边界条件
$$B_{1n} = B_{2n}, \quad H_{1t} - H_{2t} = K \quad (\text{surface current / 面电流})$$

## 6.9 Inductance / 电感
$$L = \frac{\lambda}{I} = \frac{N\Phi}{I}, \quad W_m = \frac{1}{2}LI^2 = \frac{1}{2}\int_V \mathbf{B}\cdot\mathbf{H}\,dV$$
