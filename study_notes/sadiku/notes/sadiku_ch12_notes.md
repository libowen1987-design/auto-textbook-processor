# Sadiku《Elements of Electromagnetics》Chapter 12: Modern Topics
> **中英双语版**

## 12.1 Microwave Communications / 微波通信
Satellite links, radar systems, cellular networks. Key concepts: link budget, Friis transmission formula $P_r = P_t G_t G_r \lambda^2/(4\pi R)^2$.

## 12.2 Radar / 雷达
Radar equation / 雷达方程: $P_r = P_t G_t^2 \lambda^2 \sigma / [(4\pi)^3 R^4]$, where $\sigma$ = RCS (radar cross section / 雷达散射截面).
Pulse radar (range), CW radar (Doppler velocity), SAR (synthetic aperture imaging).

## 12.3 Electromagnetic Compatibility / 电磁兼容 (EMC)
EMI (electromagnetic interference / 电磁干扰) sources: digital circuits, motors, lightning.
Shielding effectiveness / 屏蔽效能: SE = $R + A + M$ (dB), where $R$ = reflection, $A$ = absorption, $M$ = multiple reflections.

## 12.4 Fiber Optics / 光纤
Step-index and graded-index fibers. Numerical aperture / 数值孔径:
$$\text{NA} = \sqrt{n_1^2 - n_2^2}$$
V-number: $V = 2\pi a \,\text{NA}/\lambda$; single-mode condition $V < 2.405$.
Attenuation (dB/km) due to absorption and scattering.

## 12.5 Biological Effects / 生物效应
SAR (Specific Absorption Rate / 比吸收率): $\text{SAR} = \sigma|E|^2/(2\rho)$ [W/kg].
Thermal effects, non-thermal effects, safety standards (FCC, ICNIRP).

## 12.6 Nanophotonics / 纳米光子学
Surface plasmon polaritons, metamaterials, negative refractive index materials.
