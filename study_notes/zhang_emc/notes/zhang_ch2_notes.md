## 2.1 Basic Knowledge of EMC | EMC基础

### 2.1.1 Common EMC Terms and Definitions | 通用EMC术语与定义

#### 2.1.1.1 EMC and Margin | EMC与裕量

**Definition of EMC (电磁兼容性定义):**
> Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) is the ability of electrical equipment or systems to function properly in their electromagnetic environment (EME) while not causing unwanted effects such as electromagnetic interference (EMI) to anything else in this environment.

**Three key concepts from the source text:**

| 术语 | 英文 | 定义 |
|------|------|------|
| **电磁发射** | EM Emission | 能量从辐射源发出的现象；沿电缆传导的称为传导发射(CE)，通过空间辐射的称为辐射发射(RE) |
| **电磁敏感性** | EM Susceptibility | 设备或系统能够正常工作的EMI水平上限；承受传导干扰的能力为CS，承受辐射干扰的能力为RS |
| **EMI裕量** | EMI Margin | 敏感度限值(susceptibility limit)与发射限值(emission limit)之间的差值 |

> **From the source:** *"The relationship among the emission limit, susceptibility limit, and the margin of equipment or a system is shown in Fig. 2.1."*

**Physical meaning:** EMI margin measures how much "buffer" exists between the maximum allowed interference a device can tolerate and the actual interference level it emits. A positive margin means the device is protected.

**Classification of equipment:**
- **EMI equipment (干扰设备):** Emits voltages, currents, or EM fields (e.g., switching power supplies, processors)
- **EM sensitive equipment (敏感设备):** Subject to EM emissions and may experience performance degradation

Some equipment like radars and transponders have both emission and reception functions, switching between modes as needed.

#### 2.1.1.2 Three Elements of EMC and Immunity Test Levels | EMC三要素与抗扰测试等级

**Three elements of EMC:**
1. **Sources of interference (干扰源)**
2. **Coupling paths (耦合路径)**
3. **Sensitive equipment (敏感设备)**

**Main tasks of EMC:**
- Identify and control EMI sources
- Cut off EMI coupling paths or increase loss in the paths
- Identify EM sensitive equipment and improve its immunity capability

**Three immunity test levels from the source:**
| Level | Definition | Physical meaning |
|-------|------------|-------------------|
| **Survival level (生存级)** | EUT/system has no permanent performance failure in specified intensity EMI environment | Device remains functional after exposure |
| **Working level (工作级)** | EUT/system does not experience malfunction, functional failure, memory change, or external intervention | Normal operation maintained |
| **Performance level (性能级)** | EUT/system reliably performs engineering tasks and meets technical specifications | Full performance maintained |

### 2.1.2 Periodic Signal Spectrum Envelope of Digital Circuits | 数字电路周期信号频谱包络

**Source text description:**
> *"A large number of digital circuits are used on spacecraft electronic equipment, which will generate periodic signals such as clocks or crystal oscillators."*

**Key parameters from the formula in the source:**
$$V_n = V_0 \frac{\sin(\pi n f_0 \tau_w)}{\pi n f_0 \tau_w} \cdot \frac{\sin(\pi n f_0 \tau_r)}{\pi n f_0 \tau_r}$$

where:
- $V_n$ = nth harmonic voltage amplitude (第n次谐波电压幅值)
- $V_0$ = signal voltage amplitude (信号电压幅值)
- $f_0 = 1/T$ = signal frequency (信号频率)
- $\tau_w$ = signal width (信号宽度, typically $T/2$)
- $\tau_r$ = signal rise time (上升时间)

**Spectrum envelope corner frequencies (频谱包络拐点频率):**
$$F_1 = \frac{1}{\pi \tau_w}, \quad F_2 = \frac{1}{\pi \tau_r}$$

**Envelope slopes:**
- $F < F_1$: $-20$ dB/decade
- $F > F_2$: $-40$ dB/decade

> **Physical insight from the source:** *"The harmonic interference spectrum generated by digital circuits is closely related to the rise time and signal width time of various clock or crystal oscillator signals, and does not depend entirely on the frequency of the signal itself."*

This is critical for spacecraft EMC: a 100 MHz clock with 1 ns rise time generates significant harmonics up to several GHz, which can interfere with RF receivers.

## 2.3 Spacecraft Electrical Bonding | 航天器电气搭接

### 2.3.1 Functions of Electrical Bonding | 电气搭接的功能

**From the source:** *"Spacecraft electrical bonding is aimed to establish a low impedance current path between two metal objects, so that the potential voltage between different equipment and structures is minimal."*

**Main functions:**
1. Providing a common potential reference for electronic equipment
2. Providing a path for the RF current of the shielding and filter
3. Providing a low impedance path for the antenna mounted externally on the spacecraft
4. Providing a current path in fault condition and preventing overheating or electric hazard

### 2.3.2 Main Classes of Electrical Bonding | 电气搭接的主要分类

**Four classes from the source:**

| Class | Purpose | DC Resistance | Frequency | Current |
|-------|---------|---------------|-----------|---------|
| **Class C** (Current Return Path Bonding) | Reduce power and voltage losses at the bonding interface | < 0.1 mΩ | Low | High |
| **Class H** (Electric Shock Hazard or Fault Protection Bonding) | Prevent fire or shock hazard to personnel | < 2.5 mΩ, low inductance | Low | High |
| **Class R** (RF Equipment Bonding or EMI Suppression) | Control the RF emissions of the equipment | < 1.0 mΩ | High | Low |
| **Class S** (Electrostatic Charging Control Bonding) | Control the effects of ESD; applicable for equipment that may be electrostatically charged | — | — | — |

**Key insight from the source:** *"Since a specific bonding may have to meet multiple application requirements, it should be implemented with the most stringent bonding requirement."*

For example, power supply and distribution equipment must meet both Class R (for RF emissions) and Class H (for fault current protection) requirements. The bonding resistance should have low inductance DC of 2.5 mΩ while the bonding area must withstand maximum fault current for Class H.

### 2.3.3 Main Methods of Electrical Bonding | 电气搭接的主要方法

**Direct Bonding (直接搭接):**
> *"Metals are bonded directly without any intermediate material to provide the lowest resistance connection."*

**Indirect Bonding (间接搭接):**
> *"Used when direct bonding is not possible; uses jumpers, straps, or conductive gaskets to maintain electrical continuity."*

### 2.3.4 Cleaning and Protection of Bonding Surfaces | 搭接表面的清洁与保护

**Important from the source:** *"The bonding surfaces shall be cleaned to ensure low resistance contact. Protective coatings should be applied to prevent corrosion while maintaining conductivity."*

### 2.3.5 Verification of Bonding | 搭接的验证

**Verification requirements from the source:**
- DC resistance measurements between bonding points
- RF impedance verification at operating frequencies
- Visual inspection of surface preparation and contact quality

## 2.4 Main Methods of EMI Interface Control | EMI接口控制的主要方法

**From the source:** *"The EMI interface control of spacecraft system should mainly focus on the grounding and bonding, cable interface, power supply characteristics, and shielding."*

### (1) Grounding | 接地

**Early purpose of grounding (from the source):**
- Prevent safety problems in case of short-circuit current over-flush
- Prevent electric charge accumulation between different metal surfaces and forming ESD
- Prevent analog sensor disturbance and control remanence magnetic properties

**Grounding methods by frequency:**
| Application | Method | Reason |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| Power supply return | Single-point grounding | Minimize ground loop of power current |
| Low frequency analog circuits | Single-point grounding at both ends of circuit and in shielded cable | Prevent ground loops |
| High frequency digital circuits | Multi-point grounding in circuit and shielding | Control transmission line effects |
| Mixed high and low frequency | Hybrid grounding | Both requirements |

### (2) Bonding | 搭接

**From the source:** *"Low-impedance connections between metal surfaces can reduce plasma charging and PIM effects, and good bonding can improve the shielding effectiveness of the cables, chassis, and spacecraft modules in high frequency bands."*

### (3) Cable Interface | 电缆接口

**Interface control from the source:**
- Determine the "interference margin" for each interface
- Establish an "interference control baseline" based on these margins
- The baseline includes: cross-modulation, reflection, ground potential variation, and external induced interference characteristics

### (4) Shielding | 屏蔽

**Design principles from the source:**
- Shielding can be applied to a single cable, a module, or an integrated system
- Considering the weight limitation of spacecraft, metal foil tape is recommended over heavy shielding structures
